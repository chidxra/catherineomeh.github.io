Festival of Eccentrics 2024

A Ballroom-style competition performance lead by students where "houses" compete for the title, House of Eccentrics.

2024 • Spriggs Burroughs Drama and Dance Ensemble • Giles 106, Spelman College

Overview

Festival of Eccentrics is a 100% student devised production. As a member of the House of Poetix (Poetry), I competed against students within the Houses of Act Awup (Acting) and Dancestravaganza (Dance). Musikal (Music) was on hiatus during the production. The houses competed in performance and runway walk catagories themed around becoming the best version of oneself, and honoring those that came before us. .

Research Questions

  • How can performance be used to educate other on figures within Yoruba mythology?
  • How can attire, movement, and props be used to represent memories shared within a community?
  • How can lighting and movement be used to enhance performances with few props?

Creative Innovation

The goals of this project are as follows:

  • To develop spoken word pieces from written works from theatrical performances.
  • To explore the use of lighting, sound, and movement to enhance performance.
  • To practice the utilization of untraditional spaces for theatrical spaces.
  • To design a memorable experience that students actively want to engage with.
